Who We Are

If you're reading this website you probably already know who we are, but just in case you're new here, I'm Emily Elizabeth Reo and that over there (currently swimming in Crystal Lake or at Anna's eating a burrito) is Julian Saltz Fader. If you happened to stumble across this URL by accident, please keep moving. Nothing to see here.

We are getting married because we love each other a lot and get along well most of the time and we want to hang out when we're old. We also like a lot of the same things, like music, animals (cats the most), eating food, recording and mixing in Logic Pro X, taking long bike rides, mid-tier sedans from the early 2000s, nature, and more. It all started with a show at Middlesex in 2013, Julian buying various items from me between 2014-2016 (I get them all back now), and a "complex courtship", eventually followed by 5 years of engagement. 9 years later, here we are!

Getting Around

Boston has good public transit, including a small but reliable subway system (called the T) and lots of buses. There is also a commuter rail that takes you to the 'burbs. We have 1 (one) car and are happy to give rides, but unfortunately cannot fit all 13 of you safely or comfortably. We recommend a combo of transit, Uber/Lyft, and carpooling when possible.

Boston is also very bike-friendly, and we have 4 (four) bikes that you can borrow from us if you'd like. If you choose to bike, we can recommend lots of protected routes and pretty bike paths. Let us know ahead of time and we'll make sure we have enough helmets and the bikes are ready for you.
